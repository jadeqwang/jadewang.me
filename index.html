MADE FOR THE SONGS

  • 01 · Music video + deep dive

    Moongazing (望明月)

    An astronaut spends thirteen months at the lunar south pole while her family looks up at the Moon from Earth. Her International Moonbase doesn't look like a movie moonbase: it's built only from technologies we'd really use, and real science experiments fill out the world. Drawn in ink, silk and gold by JavaScript.

    moongazing.cc ↗

  • 02 · Music video + deep dive

    Orbital Sunrise

    Alexei Leonov's first spacewalk and the hand-flown landing of Voskhod-2. The equations around his head are real, for anyone who pauses to read them: as close as we could get to what he was computing once the guidance system failed, from a reconstruction of the orbit built on mission logs, Leonov's memoirs and other primary sources. Every frame drawn in colored pencil by JavaScript.

    orbitalsunrise.cc ↗

About

Jade Q Wang

Jade Q Wang is a musician, poet, martial artist and scientist. She started classical piano before three, played violin in elementary and middle school, and taught herself guitar. Since then: PhD in neuroscience, postdoc at NASA Ames, took hacker houses mainstream, and a decade in open-source software at Sandstorm and Cloudflare.

Now she's writing Rare Earth one song at a time, including reviving some 2011–2013 songs from her band, RNA (Robot Ninja Apocalypse). Some songs are adapted from poems from Spaces Left Blank, her science fiction poetry memoirs, a playable experience inside the context of a Claude conversation. She also plays video games with her husband Kenton Varda and their kids in Austin.
