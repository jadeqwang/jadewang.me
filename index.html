About

Jade Q Wang

Jade Q Wang is a musician, poet, martial artist and scientist. She started classical piano before she was three, played violin in elementary and middle school, and taught herself guitar. Since then she's earned a PhD in neuroscience, done a postdoc at NASA Ames, taken hacker houses mainstream, and spent a decade building open-source software at Sandstorm and Cloudflare. Now she's writing Rare Earth one song at a time.

She plays video games with her husband Kenton and their kids in Austin.
